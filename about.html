About ORG Manager

Empowering local organizations through smart management

What is ORG Manager?

ORG Manager is a comprehensive Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) system designed specifically for local organizations, community groups, and student chapters. Our platform streamlines financial management, member coordination, project tracking, and resource allocation in one centralized system.

Our Mission

We believe that every organization, regardless of size, deserves access to professional-grade management tools. ORG Manager makes it easy for community leaders to focus on what matters most: serving their members and making a positive impact.

Key Features

💰

Financial Management

Track income, expenses, and budgets with real-time reporting and approval workflows

👥

Member Directory

Maintain organized records of members, roles, and contact information

📋

Project Management

Manage equipment, facilities, and shared assets efficiently
